# Why use interactive audio?

## The most basic and overarching reason is to increase the 'juice' of your game 

### Whether we are conscious of it or not, when an element in a game reacts to our actions, we are more immersed. 

###  This can be tied to the psychological concept of operant conditioning: 

![[stormcastle-tv.gif]]
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MGpFEv1-mAo 

---
## What sorts of behaviors can we enforce with interactive audio?

### When we design games, we want to guide the player towards certain actions.  

### For example, in a game about speedrunning, we want to encourage the player to go fast. 

### When I designed the interactive music for Moonset, I added new instrument stems as the player went faster

![[stormcastle-tv.gif]] 

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fo476ePbalE

---
## We can even make this a core element of the game
### Games with rhythm elements often use interactivity to indicate how well the player is doing, or to describe aspects of the game state.
