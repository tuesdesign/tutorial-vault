## Backmasking

### Prior to the widespread adoption of digital mixing, most studio recorded music was written to large reel-to-reel tapes, and tracks were combined in a process called masking

### It's possible to put the tape in backwards, creating a reversed effect (hence, backmasking)
![[Pasted image 20261110131437.png | 300]]

## Slow-Scan Waves

### SSTV (slow scan television) is a type of radio transmission used to encode images

### It works by assigning colour values to specific frequencies, with a set duration for each pixel 

### This is the technique that was used to transmit live footage of the moon landing back to earth

![[Pasted image 20261110132635.png]]
