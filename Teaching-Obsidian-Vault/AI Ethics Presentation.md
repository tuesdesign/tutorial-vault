## A cautionary tale

##### In 2016, Microsoft created 'Tay', a Twitter page that hosted what was arguably the first AI influencer.
##### It used an early text model that 'learned' terms and phrases from its online text conversations - any twitter user could message it.  

![[Pasted image 20260225055740.png | 400]]

---
<!-- slide bg="grad-006.jpg" -->
#### A group of internet trolls coordinated to expose 'Tay' to hateful content, hoping that it would reproduce it.

##### Within 24 hours, Tay begun to produce hate speech, slurs, and extremist political rhetoric. As a result, Microsoft deactivated the account 

![[Pasted image 20260225060248.png | 400]]
