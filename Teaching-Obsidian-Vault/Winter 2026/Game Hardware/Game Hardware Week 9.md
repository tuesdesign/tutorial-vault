# FDM Printing

### Fused Deposition Modelling 

![[Pasted image 20260319142532.png | 300]]

### The most common type of 3D Printer, melts and deposits filament onto a build plate in thin layers

### Advantages: 
- #### Accessible, Cheap, many material choices, fast prototyping
### Disadvantages
- #### Visible layer lines, low shear strength

---
# SLA Printing
### Stereolithography

![[Pasted image 20260319143028.png | 300]]

### A display emits UV light which cures a thin layer of resin onto a build plate

### Advantages
 - ### Extremely high detail, transparency, uniform strength, even faster than FDM
 ### Disadvantages
 - ### Lower dimensional accuracy, expensive materials, required washing and curing of parts

---
# SLS
### Selective Laser Sintering
![[Pasted image 20260319145903.png | 300]]
### A laser melts thin layers of fine powdered material 

### Advantages
- ### High strength, zero waste / supports, high speed for volume applications, high detail, cool materials (even metal)

### Disadvantages
- ### Expensive, grainy and porous finish, expensive!
